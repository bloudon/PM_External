# Construction photo sources

The five jobsite photos used by the marketing site are illustrative stock photography, **not** Permits Manager customer projects. The source images were downloaded from Pexels and resized/cropped to local 480px and 960px WebP derivatives (3:2), with metadata stripped. No other photographs are added by this change.

Pexels lists each selected image as a **Free download** with the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The license permits free commercial website use and modification without mandatory attribution, but prohibits implying a pictured person or brand endorses the product. Source pages and the license were checked on October 26, 2026.
