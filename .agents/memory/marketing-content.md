# Public content boundaries

**Why:** The owner asked, “can we used zoomed in shots of existing screen shots on product images.”

**How to apply:** Use genuine cropped views, redact identifying records, and label crops accurately. Request additional genuine screenshots for unsupported visual claims.

**Why:** Legal publication is owner-managed pending approved copy.

**How to apply:** Do not replace the current legal pages with fabricated approved terms during unrelated content work. Ask for owner-approved text before changing them.
